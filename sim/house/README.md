# CMA house — a real house for testing PRIMUS

A simulation environment of the **CMA House** (Jacobsen Arquitetura, Angra dos Reis, 2018; 720 m² on a 1,609 m²
hillside plot that falls straight into the sea). It was rebuilt from the plans, the section and the 15 photographs
that ArchDaily published (photos by Fernando Guerra). It covers three levels, with furniture, kitchens with drawers
and doors that open, glazed pivot doors, a pool, decks, gardens and the glass inclined lift to the parking. It is
generated by code, so the same source produces the **MuJoCo** scene (robot locomotion) and a **USD** stage
(Isaac Sim, cameras and perception).

![preview](../models/house/previews/aerial_sea.jpg)

*(previews are generated by `render_views.py` and are not in Git)*

## Quick start

```bash
cd sim
python3 house/build.py                       # ~15 s; downloads ~200 MB of CC0 textures the first time
python3 scripts/run_xbox_viewer.py --house --spawn varanda      # drive it with a gamepad
python3 scripts/run_quest_viewer.py --world house --spawn upper_lounge   # Quest 3 / browser, D435i + lidar
python3 scripts/run_viewer.py --house        # just look (zero command)
python3 house/test_house.py                  # physics checks (11 scenarios, ~45 s)
python3 house/render_views.py                # photo-matched preview images -> sim/models/house/previews/
python3 house/build.py --quality full --usd  # dense vegetation + USD for Isaac (sim/isaac/usd/house/)
```

The scene is `sim/models/cena_casa_cma.xml` (robot + house + keyframes). Every keyframe is a spawn point, with the
robot parked:

| keyframe | where | level z |
|---|---|---|
| `parked` (key 0), `home` | living room, facing the TV wall | 0.0 |
| `varanda`, `kitchen`, `lawn`, `pool_deck` | social level | 0.0 |
| `sea_deck`, `pavilion` | sea deck / games pavilion | −3.4 |
| `upper_lounge`, `suite1`, `garden`, `terrace`, `lift_entry` | bedroom level / entrance garden | 3.6 |
| `parking` | top of the inclined lift | 13.2 |

## What is in it

| level | z (m) | spaces |
|---|---|---|
| pier | −5.4 | boat landing on timber posts, 12-step stair, moored runabout |
| sea deck | −3.4 | weathered teak deck with palms through it, hammock net with orange cushions, long bench, lounge chairs, hammocks, a 0.20 m lower step; **pavilion** under the planted roof (sauna, massage, games room with TV, foosball and a bar) |
| social | 0.0 | 3.1 × 16 m infinity pool (bench + steps), pool deck with loungers and parasols, lawn with staked trees; **varanda** (built-in sofa, timber-block tables, rope tub chairs, gourmet kitchen with island and stools), 10 glazed pivot doors, **double-height living room** (TV wall with shelves, sectionals, dining for 8 under wicker pendants, cantilevered timber stair with no railing, as built), galley kitchen, laundry, powder rooms, pantry, wine cellar, service spiral stair |
| bedrooms | 3.6 | 3 suites (king beds, wardrobes, bathrooms; freestanding tub in the master), corridor behind a timber slat screen, lounge over the kitchen (long table, sofa), study, **entrance garden** (pavers in white gravel, reflecting pool, frangipani, gingers, arecas), terrace |
| roof | 6.5–7.45 | thin hipped standing-seam roof, timber soffit, skylights |
| parking | 13.2 | glass inclined lift (39°, 15.3 m), carport, car, yellow standby generator, 20 % driveway, street at 16.1 |

Around it: the hillside (heightfield), shore boulders, four neighbouring houses with clay-tile roofs, Atlantic-forest
trees, palms and bamboo, the sea, and the islands and mountains of the bay on the horizon, under a CC0 HDRI sky.

**Moving parts (121 bodies):** 22 glazed pivot doors (varanda/living room and garden), 20 hinged interior doors,
3 sliding glass panels, ~45 kitchen and bar drawers, cabinet doors, 3 dishwashers, an oven, the fridge, and the lift
cabin with its 2 gates. They collide with the robot only (not with the house), have damping and friction, and
start in the positions of the photos (pivot doors open).

**Inclined lift** (`house_runtime.py`, called each step by the viewers): drive into the cabin from the terrace and
stop for 2 s. The gate closes, the cabin climbs at 0.6 m/s and the upper gate opens at the parking. Approaching the
station without the cabin calls it. When nobody is near, it goes back down to cover the rail pit.

## How it was reconstructed

| from the sources | measured / taken | extrapolated |
|---|---|---|
| floor plans (scale bar, 16.2 px/m) | footprints of both levels: pool 3.1 × 16 m, varanda column grid 4.1 m, pivot-door line, 9.8 × 12.2 m living room, 3 suites of 10.1 m depth, central stair 1.9 m wide, service spiral stair, reflecting pool, slanted north boundary | the two plans are aligned on the central stair and on the spiral stair (both agree to < 0.4 m) |
| section (10.2 px/m) | levels: sea → pier 0.9 m, → deck 2.9 m, → social 6.3 m; bedrooms +3.6 m; roof; the lift rises 9.6 m over 11.9 m to the parking | the deck level is used for the pavilion floor |
| text (architects, e-architect) | access by the "inclined plane" to the upper level, reflecting pool + internal garden at the entrance, double-height living room, single-storey varanda, pool and yard on the social level, spa + entertainment room near the sea access | room assignment of the service block, pavilion layout |
| photographs | materials (teak slatted ceilings, vertical timber panelling, white boards, light granite and dark porcelain floors, rubble stone walls, grey teak decks), the pivot doors, the stair, the furniture of the varanda, living room, gourmet kitchen, pool deck and sea deck, the glass lift cabin | bedrooms, bathrooms, internal kitchen, service rooms, study, cellar, pavilion interior: in the same style |

The reference images are kept outside the repository (`biped_project/references/cma_house/`, copyright of the
architects/photographer). Nothing from them is redistributed. The textures are CC0 photo scans from
[ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com) and the sky is the CC0 HDRI *Simon's Town Rocks* from
[Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com).

## Physics and sensors

- **Collision** is made of primitives (boxes, cylinders, spheres) that follow the solid volumes: floors, walls with
  their openings, each stair tread, glass, rails, furniture bodies, tree trunks. The hillside is a 0.5 m heightfield.
  Visual meshes (group 1) have no collision. Static colliders: `contype 1 / conaffinity 1`; moving parts
  `4 / 2` (they touch the robot, `2 / 1`, but not the house).
- Colliders are in **geom group 4**: hidden in renders and in the robot's depth camera, but visible with the
  group-4 toggle in the MuJoCo viewer. The simulated RPLIDAR casts against groups 0 and 4 (`skill_world.SimSensors`).
  `mj_ray` ignores alpha-0 geoms, which is why the colliders are not invisible group-0 geoms.
- Friction (MuJoCo sliding coefficient): polished stone 0.75, timber floor 0.85, teak deck 0.95, lawn 0.8,
  gravel 0.7, concrete 1.0, glass 0.45, pool mosaic 0.6.
- **Challenges for the robot**: the living-room stair (22 × 16.4 cm risers, 43 cm treads, open on one side, no
  railing), three outdoor stone stairs (16.2–17.1 cm risers), the pier stair, the 0.20 m deck step, a 12 mm rug edge,
  thresholds of 0 to 30 mm, the pool (1.4 m deep), the reflecting pool (0.3 m), the lift pit when the cabin is away,
  narrow bathroom doors, glass you can bump into, pivot doors you can push open (the robot pushes one to 48° in the
  test), and polished floors.
- The robot runs at about **3.2× real time** on the CPU with the model-based controller (`test_house.py`).

## Performance profiles

| | `--quality realtime` (default) | `--quality full` |
|---|---|---|
| static triangles | 0.84 M | 3.1 M |
| render 1280×720 (RTX 5070, EGL) | ~210 fps | ~12 fps |
| vegetation | 70 trees, 110 shrub masses, 3-segment leaves | 150 trees, 260 masses, full leaves |
| water mirrors | pool, reflecting pool | + the sea |

Use `realtime` for piloting (MuJoCo viewer, Quest streaming, perception at 15 Hz). Use `full` for stills, videos
and the Isaac export.

## Isaac Sim

`build.py --usd` writes `sim/isaac/usd/house/cma_house.usd` (Z up, metres, the same frame). It contains:
- UsdPreviewSurface materials with colour, normal and roughness maps;
- merged visual meshes;
- invisible primitive colliders with UsdPhysics friction materials;
- the terrain as a triangle-mesh collider;
- the 121 moving parts as rigid bodies with revolute/prismatic joints (the lift has a position drive);
- an HDRI dome light, the sun, interior sphere lights;
- the 15 photo-matched cameras;
- the spawn points.

Load the robot (`sim/isaac/usd/robo_bipedal.usd`) and place it at a `/World/Spawns/*` prim. The stage was validated
with `pxr` only (123 joints, 839 colliders, all 123 texture paths resolve). **It has not been opened in Isaac Sim
yet**, so the dome orientation and the light intensities may need a tweak there.
